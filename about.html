About All MCQ 360

Free MCQs with the simplest explanations.

What is All MCQ 360?

All MCQ 360 is a free website for practising multiple-choice questions for sarkari (government) exams. It covers 20 subjects with 100 MCQs each — 2,000 questions in total.

Pick a subject, then click an answer for each question. A green option means correct, a red option means wrong, and the correct answer is shown right away. Every question has a short, simple explanation written in plain English, so you understand the answer instead of just memorising it.

Subjects covered

  • Quantitative Aptitude, Logical Reasoning, General Knowledge, Current Affairs and English
  • Biology, Hindi Language & Grammar, Physics, Chemistry and Child Development & Pedagogy
  • Computer Awareness, Indian Economy, Environment & Ecology, Art & Culture, Sports and Banking Awareness
  • History, Geography, Polity and Science

Free, always

All 2,000 MCQs are free to use. There are no accounts, no logins and no fees — just open a subject and start practising.
